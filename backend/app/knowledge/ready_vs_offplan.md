# Ready versus Off-Plan Property

General information only. Trade-offs are general, not a
recommendation for a particular customer.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"Ready property-na, already mudinjirukku, neenga poi paarunga,
key eduthukonga. Off-plan-na, innum kattitu irukku, so price and
choice konjam different-ah irukkum, aana wait pannanum. Off-plan-la
escrow framework irukku, adhu oru safeguard. Aana adhu completion
guarantee illa nga. Edhu ungalukku suit aagum-nu neenga dhaan
decide pannanum."

## The difference in plain terms
- Ready (completed) unit: the building is finished. What you see is
  largely what you get, and the transaction is a transfer of an
  existing unit.
- Off-plan unit: bought before the project is finished. The project
  must be registered with DLD and the money goes through the escrow
  framework (see the escrow topic).

## Honest trade-offs to mention as questions to consider
- Choice and price for a completed unit versus an unfinished
  project: usually a different selection and a different price
  point, but this project has not verified any specific figure.
- Waiting time: off-plan involves waiting for completion.
- Risk: off-plan carries completion, delay and market risk that a
  completed unit does not carry in the same way.
- Payment plan: off-plan purchases are commonly tied to a payment
  plan; a completed purchase is commonly a one-time payment.

## Common misconceptions and limits
- That off-plan is always cheaper. Never claim this.
- That escrow guarantees completion. It does not.
- That a project's advertised handover date is a certainty.
- That our catalogue shows live availability for either type. It
  does not.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Edhu risk kammi?" - explain that risk differs in kind, not in a
  simple ranking, and that neither is risk-free.
- "Rental income eppo start aagum?" - depends on handover, which is
  not verified here.

## General versus case-specific
General: the nature of each option and the kind of trade-off
involved.
Case-specific: whether a named project will be delivered on time,
and how a specific unit is priced today.
