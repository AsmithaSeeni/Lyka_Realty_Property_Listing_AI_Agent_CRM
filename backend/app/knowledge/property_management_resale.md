# Property Management, Renting Out and Resale

General information only, and this project has NOT verified the
conditions that attach to a specific contract.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"Property vaanginadhu-kku aprom, adha neenga use pannalaam, illa
rent-ku kudukkalaam. Rent-ku kudutha, DLD-la tenancy register
pannanum, and DLD standard brokerage forms vachirukku. Sell
panna vendum-na, adhu DLD-la transfer dhaan. Aana oru
project-oda contract-la resale condition irukkum - adha contract-la
padikkalaam, naan assume panna maatten."

## What is verified
- DLD's Rules & Regulations section publishes a Tenancy Guide and
  standard leasing brokerage agreement forms, including an
  agreement between owner and broker, one between broker and
  tenant, and a property viewing agreement. Leasing is therefore a
  documented area of DLD practice.
- DLD's rules include jointly-owned property material and
  circulars about real estate management companies and about
  changing a jointly-owned property management company, so
  professional building management is a recognised arrangement.
- DLD rules reference a Rental Disputes Center in the context of
  service-fee collection.
- A resale or transfer of ownership is registered with DLD.

## What is NOT verified in this project
- Whether a particular project's contract restricts resale before
  handover, and any transfer condition or fee attached to it.
- Any rental figure, occupancy rate or management fee.
- The exact steps to register a tenancy today, or any associated
  charge.
- Any rule on short-term letting.

Never assert a resale condition. Say it is written in the
contract and must be read there.

## Common misconceptions and limits
- That an off-plan unit can always be resold freely before
  handover. The contract decides.
- That renting out is purely the owner's private arrangement with
  no registration or formality.
- That service charges disappear when the unit is rented.
- That a management company can be replaced freely; DLD has a
  circular about changing a jointly-owned property management
  company, so there is a process.
- That a dispute is settled by the developer.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Rent-ku kudutha evlo varum?" - unverified; never quote a rent.
- "Resale pannalaama?" - read the contract terms; confirm rather
  than assert.
- "Management yaaru paakkum?" - explain that professional building
  management is normal for jointly-owned property and that DLD
  publishes material about it.

## General versus case-specific
General: that leasing, management and resale all sit inside DLD's
documented framework.
Case-specific: the terms of one contract and the numbers for one
unit.
