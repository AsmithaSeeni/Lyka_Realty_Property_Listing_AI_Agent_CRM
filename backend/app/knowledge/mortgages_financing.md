# Mortgages and Financing Considerations

General information only. This project has NOT verified any
current rate, loan-to-value limit, fee or eligibility rule.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"Mortgage-nga, adhu bank decide panradhu. Interest rate, evlo
percent loan, eligibility - ellam bank-a poruthu maarum, and
resident-a illa non-resident-a-ngaradhu-kku kooda maarum. Adhu
naan memory-la irundhu solla maatten nga. Ungalukku interest
irundha, naan adha confirm panna help panren."

## What is verified
- DLD's website publishes information aimed at financial
  institutions as one of its audience sections, so financing is a
  recognised part of the Dubai property market.
- A mortgaging bank's deposit into a project escrow account is
  referenced in DLD's escrow service terms, which shows financing
  interacts with the escrow process for off-plan projects.

## What is NOT verified in this project
- Any interest rate, margin or benchmark.
- Any loan-to-value percentage, minimum income, or maximum age
  rule.
- Whether a non-resident or a foreign national can obtain a
  mortgage at all, or on what terms.
- Pre-approval timelines, bank fees or insurance requirements.

Never quote a rate or a limit. Say it is a bank decision and offer
to have the specific question checked.

## Common misconceptions and limits
- That a rate seen in an advertisement is available to everyone.
- That pre-approval guarantees the loan.
- That an off-plan unit can always be mortgaged, or that it cannot:
  confirm rather than assert.
- That financing removes risk. It adds an obligation to repay.
- That a bank's valuation equals the purchase price.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Rate evlo?" - must come from a bank; do not guess.
- "Naan India-la irundhu mortgage vaangalaama?" - confirm with the
  bank; do not assume.
- "Cash-ah, loan-ah better?" - explain the trade-off in general
  terms without recommending.

## General versus case-specific
General: that financing exists and is decided by the lender.
Case-specific: the customer's own eligibility, rate and terms.
