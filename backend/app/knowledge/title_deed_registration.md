# Title Deeds, Sale Registration and Buyer Documents

General information only. Confirm the document checklist and fees
for the specific transaction with DLD.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"Dubai-la registration dhaan important nga. Ready unit-na,
ownership DLD-la register pannitu title deed varum. Off-plan-na,
contract Oqood system-la register aagum. Ungaluku exact-a enna
documents venum, evlo fees - adhu deal-a poruthu maarum, adha
DLD or unga lawyer confirm pannanum."

## What is verified
- DLD is the authority for land and property registration in
  Dubai.
- DLD's Oqood portal is referenced in DLD's own escrow service
  procedure (the developer logs in to the Oqood portal), so Oqood
  is DLD's system for off-plan contracts.
- DLD's Rules & Regulations section publishes real estate
  legislation and circulars, and separately lists UAE
  anti-money-laundering legislation, which is part of real estate
  practice in Dubai.

## What is NOT verified in this project
- The exact document checklist for a transfer.
- The current fee amounts, fee percentages or service times for
  registration.
- The precise sequence of steps for a specific off-plan or ready
  transaction.

Never quote a registration fee percentage or a document list from
memory.

## Common misconceptions and limits
- That paying the seller is the same as becoming the registered
  owner. Registration with DLD is what records ownership.
- That a developer's internal record is the same as a DLD title
  deed.
- That documents for one nationality or one transaction type will
  match another.
- That anti-money-laundering checks do not apply. DLD publishes
  UAE AML legislation among its real estate rules, so expect
  identity and source-of-funds checks and tell the customer to
  confirm the specifics.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Enna documents venum?" - say the checklist depends on the
  transaction and must be confirmed with DLD, then offer to have
  the specific requirement checked.
- "Title deed eppo kidaikkum?" - after registration, but give no
  timeline from memory.
- "India-la irundhu documents anuppalaama?" - see the
  buying-from-India topic and recommend a professional.

## General versus case-specific
General: that ownership is registered with DLD and that AML checks
form part of real estate practice.
Case-specific: the document list, the fees and the timing for this
particular purchase.
