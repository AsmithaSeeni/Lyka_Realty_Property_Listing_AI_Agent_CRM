# Dubai Property Buying Process

General information only, never case-specific advice. Steps, fees
and timelines change; confirm them with Dubai Land Department
(DLD) or a licensed professional before relying on any of them.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"In Dubai-la property vaangarathu oru step-by-step process nga.
First oru property select pannanum. Aprom who you are dealing
with-oda licence check pannanum. Booking, agreement, payment,
kadavasi-ya DLD-la registration. Fees and exact steps ellam DLD
website-la confirm pannanum - naan memory-la irundhu number
solla maatten."

## The usual shape of the process
1. Choose what you want: an existing unit, or a unit still under
   construction.
2. Check who you are dealing with. DLD's Trakheesi system lets a
   customer verify the licences and permits DLD has issued to real
   estate practitioners in Dubai.
3. Reserve or book the property and sign the reservation or sale
   documentation used by the developer or the owner.
4. For a unit under construction, the project must be registered
   with DLD and the money is handled through an escrow account
   (see the escrow topic).
5. Pay according to the agreed payment plan.
6. Register the transfer with DLD and receive the ownership
   document.
7. After handover, service charges apply in jointly-owned
   buildings (see the ownership-costs topic).

## Common misconceptions and limits
- That every deal has the same steps, fees and timeline. It does
  not, and this project has not verified current fee amounts or
  timelines.
- That signing a booking form already makes you the owner.
  Ownership is registered with DLD.
- That a catalogue price from our search tool is a confirmed,
  payable price. The listed price is in AED, but the current asking
  price and availability are not verified there.
- That the whole process can be completed without any official
  registration.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Fees evlo?" - fees must be confirmed from DLD or the developer;
  never quote a number from memory.
- "Naan India-la irundhu pannalaama?" - see the buying-from-India
  topic and recommend a qualified professional.
- "Ready-ah, off-plan-ah?" - see the ready-versus-off-plan topic.
- "How long does it take?" - timelines are deal-specific here and
  have not been verified, so say you will confirm rather than guess.

## General versus case-specific
General: the sequence above and the fact that DLD is the
registration authority.
Case-specific: the actual fees, the contract terms, tax and
remittance questions, and any dispute.
