# Payment Plans, Booking Deposits and Instalments

General information only. This project has not verified any
current percentage, deposit rule or instalment schedule.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"Payment plan-nga, adhu developer-a poruthu maarum. Booking
deposit, aprom instalments - adhu project-oda plan-la irukkum. DLD
escrow service-la kooda project payment plan dhaan required
document-a irukku. Enna percentage, evlo deposit - adha naan
memory-la irundhu solla maatten nga; andha project-oda plan-a
paakalaam."

## What is verified
- DLD's escrow account activation service lists the project
  payment plan among its required documents, so a project payment
  plan is a real, DLD-referenced document for off-plan projects.
- DLD's escrow service terms describe money being held and
  released under a regulated process.

## What is NOT verified in this project
- Any deposit percentage, instalment percentage or number of
  instalments.
- Any handover-linked payment milestone.
- Any fee charged by a developer or broker.
- Whether a plan is negotiable.

Never state a percentage or a schedule from memory. If the
customer needs it, say it must come from the project's own
payment plan.

## Common misconceptions and limits
- That every developer uses the same plan. They do not.
- That a low booking deposit means low total cost. It does not.
- That a payment plan removes completion or market risk. It does
  not.
- That the plan is a guarantee the project finishes.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Booking deposit evlo?" - must come from the specific project;
  do not guess.
- "Instalments evlo varusham?" - project-specific, unverified.
- "Escrow-la dhaan money poguma?" - yes for the relevant off-plan
  arrangement, and it is a regulated safeguard, not a completion
  guarantee. See the escrow topic.

## General versus case-specific
General: that a project payment plan exists, is referenced by
DLD's escrow process, and that deposits and instalments are
normal.
Case-specific: the actual numbers, which belong to the project's
own plan and the signed agreement.
