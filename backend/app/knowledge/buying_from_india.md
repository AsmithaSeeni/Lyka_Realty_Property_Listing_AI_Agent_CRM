# Buying from India or Another Country

General information only. This is explicitly NOT Indian tax, FEMA,
remittance, immigration or legal advice. Route those questions to a
qualified professional.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"India-la irundhu vaangarathu possible nga, aana rendu pakkam
paakkanum. UAE pakkam, identity and source-of-funds checks
irukkum - AML rules ellam real estate-la apply aagum. India
pakkam, remittance and tax rules - adhu naan solla koodadhu nga,
adhu oru qualified CA or lawyer paakkanum. Naan neraya
assumptions solli ungalukku problem kudukka maatten."

## What is verified
- DLD's Rules & Regulations section publishes UAE
  anti-money-laundering legislation among its real estate rules.
  This is a real regulatory area for UAE property.
- Expect identity and source-of-funds checks in real estate
  transactions.

## What is NOT verified in this project
- All Indian-side rules: FEMA, remittance channels, TCS or any
  tax treatment, reporting obligations, and whether a
  certificate or approval is needed.
- UAE residency or visa consequences of buying.
- Whether a buyer must travel, and whether a power of attorney can
  be used. Confirm with DLD or a lawyer.
- Any double-taxation or capital-gains treatment.

Never answer any of these from memory. Say plainly that they need
a qualified professional, and that you do not want to give a
wrong answer.

## Practical guidance Priya can give safely
- The purchase itself is registered in Dubai through DLD's
  systems, so the buyer does not have to rely on a verbal promise
  from a seller.
- Verify the licence of whoever is dealing with the money, using
  DLD's licence verification service.
- For off-plan, the escrow framework applies (see the escrow
  topic) - and it is a safeguard, not a completion guarantee.
- Keep documents in the buyer's own name and get proper receipts.

## Common misconceptions and limits
- "I can send money however I like and it will be fine." Not
  something to assert; the customer must check their own rules.
- "Buying gives me a visa." Never claim this.
- "The developer's lawyer protects me." Their duty may not be to
  the buyer; recommend independent advice.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Enna documents venum?" - a general check of identity and
  source of funds, with the exact list confirmed with DLD.
- "Tax irukka?" - refer to a qualified professional.
- "Naan India-la irundhu epdi pay panradhu?" - referral, not
  advice.

## General versus case-specific
General: that AML checks exist in UAE real estate and that DLD
registration protects ownership.
Case-specific: everything about the buyer's own country's rules
and their personal tax position.
