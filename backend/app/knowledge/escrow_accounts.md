# Escrow Accounts for Off-Plan Property

General information only. Never present escrow as a guarantee.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"Off-plan-nga, adhu innum kattitu irukkura project. Andha
maadhiri project-la, DLD framework-la escrow account irukkanum.
Developer-oda money adha suttu dhaan use pannanum, and DLD
technical report paathu dhaan disbursement nadakkum. Idhu oru
important safeguard nga. Aana escrow-nu sonna, project kandippa
complete aagum-nu guarantee kidaiyadhu. Adhu naan clear-ah
sollanum."

## What is verified on DLD's own service page
- DLD operates an "Escrow Account activation" service. Its own
  description: "This service allows a real estate developer to
  apply for activating the disbursement mechanism from the escrow
  account of the off-plan sold real estate project."
- It is a service used by the developer, through DLD's systems, and
  the procedure refers to logging in to the Oqood portal and to the
  trustee account reviewing and uploading documents.
- The required documents listed include the project payment plan.
- The stated service terms include: the developer must comply with
  the project's financial risk standards; a recent DLD technical
  report (not older than three months) with no red flags in the
  final stages of project progress; activation linked to a bank
  guarantee until 20 percent completion; amounts the developer
  deposits; amounts a mortgaging bank deposits; and, where there is
  financial solvency, disbursement based on a percentage of
  construction work after 20 percent completion.
- DLD states the service fee is free and the service time is three
  business days, and lists the channels as the DLD website (Real
  Estate Developers Portal, Contracts) and the Trust Account
  System.
- DLD's Rules & Regulations section lists a circular titled
  "Compliance with the Provisions of Law No. (8) of 2007
  Concerning Real Estate Development Escrow Accounts in Dubai".
  This is the escrow framework for real estate development in
  Dubai.
- DLD lists a related service for a customer to view the list of
  escrow account trustees approved by DLD.

## Common misconceptions and limits
- That escrow means the project will definitely be completed. It
  does not. Escrow is a regulated control over how money is held
  and released; it does not remove project, market or developer
  risk.
- That the money sits in a general government account. It does
  not. It is held under a regulated escrow arrangement, with
  disbursement rules and an approved trustee, and the activation
  service described above is a developer service. Never tell a
  customer their money is deposited directly into a government
  account.
- That escrow applies to every property transaction. The framework
  described by DLD concerns real estate development projects sold
  off-plan. A resale of a completed unit is a different procedure.
- That any specific percentage or step above applies to every
  project. The DLD page describes general service terms, and the
  build stage reached and the project's own documents decide what
  applies.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Money safe-ah?" - explain that the regulated escrow and
  disbursement process is a real safeguard, then say clearly that
  it is not a completion guarantee, and offer to point them to
  DLD's own information.
- "Trustee yaaru?" - DLD publishes the list of approved escrow
  account trustees; do not name one from memory.
- "Payment plan eppadi?" - see the payment-plans topic.

## General versus case-specific
General: how the escrow framework works and what it protects.
Case-specific: whether a particular project's account is active,
what its disbursement milestones are, or what happens if
construction stops. Those must be checked with DLD or a qualified
professional.
