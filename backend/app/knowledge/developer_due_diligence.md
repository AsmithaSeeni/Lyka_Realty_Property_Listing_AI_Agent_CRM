# Developer and Project Due Diligence

General information only. Priya helps the customer check facts; she
does not vouch for any developer.

## Short spoken explanation (Tanglish)
"Oru property-la interest irundha, first check pannanum nga -
andha broker-oda licence, developer-oda project registration.
DLD-la licence verify panna oru service irukku. Off-plan-na,
escrow account and trustee pathi kekkanum. Marketing brochure-a
mattum nambalaama - adhu illa. Neenga verify pannitu dhaan
decide pannanum."

## What is verified
- DLD provides a service that lets a customer verify the licences
  and permits DLD has issued to real estate practitioners in
  Dubai, through the Trakheesi system. The service time is stated
  as immediate.
- For off-plan projects, DLD's framework involves a registered
  project and a regulated escrow account, with approved trustees
  whose list DLD publishes.

## A safe checklist to walk through with the customer
1. Verify the licence of the broker and the company you are
   dealing with, using DLD's verification service.
2. For a project under construction, ask whether the project is
   registered with DLD and whether the escrow account exists.
3. Ask which trustee holds the escrow account, and cross-check
   against DLD's published list of approved trustees.
4. Read the contract itself, including the payment plan and the
   completion date terms.
5. Keep every payment traceable, and get receipts.
6. Get independent legal advice for the contract.

## Common misconceptions and limits
- That a developer's reputation removes the need to check.
- That a marketing phrase such as an approval claim is the same as
  a verified registration. Ask for the details and verify them.
- That escrow means the project will be delivered on time.
- That our catalogue confirms anything about a project. It does
  not.
- That Priya can confirm a developer's track record. She cannot, and
  must not imply it.

## Questions the customer may ask next
- "Indha developer nalla-va?" - do not rank developers; offer the
  verification steps instead.
- "Project registered-ah?" - explain how to check, and that Priya
  cannot confirm it from here.
- "Trustee yaaru?" - DLD publishes the approved list.

## General versus case-specific
General: the checklist above and the fact that verification is
possible through DLD.
Case-specific: whether a named project or developer is registered,
licensed or reliable today.
